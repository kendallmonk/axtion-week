The Blocks

The Stops

2:30  ·  Monday and Wednesday
5:30  ·  Tuesday
4:00  ·  Thursday
12:00  ·  Friday and Saturday
10:00  ·  Every night

The stops are stops. A block that overruns takes its time from the family, because there's nowhere else it can come from.

Trading

The template holds. What moves is when the hours happen, not how many.

A weekday morning can be traded — good surf, a swell that won't repeat, anything worth having
The hours come back the same week, out of Friday or Saturday morning, or they don't come back at all. No carrying, no accruing
The cost should be felt at the time — a traded morning is paid for out of life admin or my own time, not out of thin air

Friday and Saturday mornings are the only genuinely flexible time in the week. Everything after midday belongs to someone else.

Reallocating

When Kendall Monk and Signal need more than their blocks hold, the blocks don't go — what's in them changes.

Thursday Block B goes to Kendall Monk and Signal first
Tuesday Block C absorbs whatever Block A and Block B couldn't
Block A never reallocates — it's already at the top of the order

The week still works its hours. They just land on different work.

Losing Hours

When the pressure is life rather than work, hours do go — in reverse priority order per Life Plan 1.2.

1  ·  Thursday Block B
2  ·  Tuesday Block C
3  ·  Block B, Monday to Wednesday
4  ·  Block A, only if the week has genuinely broken
Traded hours come back the same week · Lost hours don't come back at all

When everything goes at once, the Minimum Viable Day in the Health & Wellbeing Plan is the floor.
